Teal + gold + cream. Geometric patterns, warm tones. Reflects the Malaysian Muslim community focus. Approachable and culturally resonant.

Dark premium base with teal + gold accents. Best of both worlds: modern dev tool feel with cultural identity. Professional yet distinctive.
