Which icon system for AmanClaw?

Replacing the current mix of Unicode symbols (⊞ ◈) and emoji (📊 🏘️) with a proper icon set

A. Lucide Icons

Thin strokes, rounded. Fork of Feather Icons with 1500+ icons. Tree-shakeable, MIT license. Clean and modern. Most popular in Svelte ecosystem.

B. Phosphor Icons

6 weight variants (thin to fill). 9000+ icons. Slightly warmer personality. Great for apps needing both outline and filled states.

C. Heroicons

By Tailwind team. 300 icons, outline + solid. Fewer options but perfectly aligned with Tailwind CSS. Sharp, geometric.

D. Custom Islamic Geometric

Hand-crafted SVGs with Islamic geometric motifs. Unique to AmanClaw. High effort, but maximum brand differentiation.

My recommendation: A (Lucide)

  • Best Svelte integration (lucide-svelte)
  • 1500+ icons covers all your pages
  • Tree-shakeable = small bundle
  • Teal/gold color applied via CSS
  • Ship fast, look professional

Why not the others

  • Phosphor: Good but heavier, less Svelte adoption
  • Heroicons: Only 300 icons — may run short
  • Custom: Weeks of work for marginal gain
